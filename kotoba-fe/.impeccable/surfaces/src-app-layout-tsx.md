## Mode
Persuade on the street hero (`/` for guests); Operate/Read on every study route (lessons, review, search, test, library, settings, legal).

## Direction contract
THESIS: Learn to read the city — kana glowing on Tokyo shop signs in the rain, read in Vietnamese.
OWN-WORLD: blue-hour street, neon sign lettering, wet-asphalt reflections, train-line light; amber is the one action colour.
STORY: see a sign, tap it, read it; then learn the kana behind every sign and drill until they are truly remembered.
FIRST VIEWPORT: rain over a skyline; real signs (ラーメン, カラオケ…) mirrored in the street; tapping one breaks it into kana with Vietnamese meaning; one framed amber action.
FORM: light-shadow-caustics-rain-night-cityscape, seed 6ba472bc. Phones unroll skyline, street and reflection into bands; answered quiz cards lock like a triggered trace; each reading keeps its ticket.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Builder amendments (phase 8, unratified: awaiting the user)
Interpretations the N5 build leans on. They are not part of the contract until the user confirms them.
- Secondary actions are ghost buttons (`.btn btnGhost`), so each view keeps exactly one amber action.
- The lesson path is drawn as a train line: lessons are stations on the elevated line from the hero.
- Study routes refuse the course-catalogue grid of cards and badges; they use rows, tables and specimens.
